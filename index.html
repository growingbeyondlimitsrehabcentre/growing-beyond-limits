ABOUT GROWING BEYOND LIMITS

Helping people participate in life.

At Growing Beyond Limits Rehab Center, occupational therapy is centred around the individual — their strengths, goals, environment and everyday occupations.

Our goal is to make therapy meaningful and practical, supporting the development of skills that can translate into everyday life, learning, participation and independence.

A few helpful things to know before getting started.

Occupational therapy supports people in developing, improving or adapting skills needed to participate in everyday activities, routines, learning, play and other meaningful occupations.
