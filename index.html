About Growing Beyond Limits

Therapy with purpose.

Helping people participate more fully in life.

At Growing Beyond Limits Rehab Center, occupational therapy focuses on helping individuals develop the abilities, confidence and strategies needed to participate in the activities that matter to them.

Therapy is designed to be meaningful, practical and individualised — connecting therapeutic goals with everyday life.

  • ✓ Individualised therapeutic goals
  • ✓ Functional and meaningful activities
  • ✓ Family-centred collaboration
  • ✓ Building confidence and independence
Occupational Therapy

Supporting growth at every step.

Therapy can support development, participation, functional skills and independence across different stages of life.

01

Developmental Support

Supporting developmental skills through purposeful, engaging and age-appropriate therapeutic activities.

02

Fine Motor Skills

Activities designed to support hand skills, coordination, dexterity and functional performance.

03

Gross Motor & Coordination

Building body awareness, coordination, balance and movement skills needed for participation.

04

Sensory Processing

Individualised strategies to support sensory regulation, participation and engagement in daily activities.

05

Activities of Daily Living

Supporting independence in everyday routines and meaningful activities at home, school and in the community.

06

Functional Skill Development

Therapy focused on practical skills that help individuals participate more confidently in everyday life.
