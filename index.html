ABOUT GROWING BEYOND LIMITS

Care with purpose.

Growing Beyond Limits Rehab Center is focused on helping individuals develop practical skills and confidence for meaningful participation in everyday life.

Our therapy approach is compassionate, individualized and centered around each person's goals, strengths and potential. We provide occupational therapy, physiotherapy and speech therapy in a supportive rehabilitation setting.

  • Individual-focused occupational therapy
  • Physiotherapy for functional mobility and movement
  • Speech therapy for communication and language skills
  • Meaningful daily-life skills
  • Confidence and participation
  • Building greater independence

Empowering potential.

Every person has potential. Our role is to create purposeful opportunities for that potential to grow into meaningful, confident participation in everyday life.
